Award-winning, accountable work

Creative Direct Marketing Group has won more than 100 marketing awards for breakthrough, profitable campaigns since its founding in 1985. Founder and CEO Craig Huey has personally been recognized with more than 126 major marketing awards across his career in direct response.

Recognized by

  • ANA International ECHO Awards — the direct marketing industry's most prestigious global recognition for results-driven campaigns.
  • Web Marketing Association WebAwards — recognizing excellence in digital and web marketing.
  • B2 Awards — honoring outstanding business-to-business marketing.
  • MarCom Awards — international recognition for excellence in marketing and communications.

Why awards matter less than results

Every one of these awards was won by a campaign that was first accountable to a number: response, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend. Awards are the recognition; measured client results are the point. That is the standard behind campaigns that scaled Alkaline88 past $70 million in sales, generated return on ad spend above 20X, and helped turn TheStreet's finances around.
