Supplement marketing that converts and complies

Supplement marketing is one of the hardest disciplines in advertising: the audience is skeptical, the regulators are watching, and the winning brands are the ones that acquire customers profitably at scale. CDMG has built customer acquisition campaigns for nutritional supplement and consumer health brands for four decades, combining persuasive direct response creative with the FDA and FTC compliance the category demands.

How CDMG grows supplement brands

  • Compliant claims architecture. Structure/function claims, required disclaimers, and substantiation standards are designed into the copy from the first draft, so campaigns persuade without making disease claims.
  • Acquisition and retention as one system. Front-end offers that acquire customers profitably, paired with continuity and retention programs that grow lifetime value for subscription products.
  • The channels that work for supplements. Direct mail and magalogs remain exceptional performers for health audiences, integrated with digital acquisition, email, and accountable media.
  • Targeting through InfoMat. Health-responsive audience data and list intelligence from our in-house data company, established in 1978.

A track record in health and nutrition

CDMG helped Health Alert grow into one of the largest publishers and supplement companies in America, and built the campaigns behind Alkaline88's growth from startup to more than $70 million in sales, with return on ad spend above 20X on multiple campaigns. The same tested, compliant, multichannel approach is available to emerging and established supplement brands alike.

Why compliance is a growth advantage

In the supplement category, non-compliant marketing is not just a legal risk; it is a business risk that ends brands. Compliant campaigns protect the company while building the specific, proof-driven messaging that skeptical health buyers respond to. Persuasion and compliance are not opposites. Done right, they compound.

Common questions

Can supplement ads make health claims?

Supplement advertising may make structure/function claims with the required FDA disclaimer, but may not claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. All claims must be substantiated to FTC standards. CDMG builds campaigns inside those rules from the first draft of copy.

What marketing channels work best for supplements?

Direct mail and magalog formats remain among the strongest performers for health audiences, integrated with digital acquisition, email, and connected TV. The right mix depends on the offer and the audience, which is why CDMG tests before scaling.

How do supplement brands acquire customers profitably?

With a tested front-end offer that covers acquisition cost, precise audience targeting, and a retention program that grows lifetime value. CDMG structures supplement campaigns around measurable cost per acquisition and long-term customer value, not impressions.

Does CDMG have supplement industry experience?

Yes. CDMG has served nutrition and supplement brands for decades, including helping Health Alert become one of the largest supplement companies in America and scaling Alkaline88 from startup past $70 million in sales.
