Privacy Policy for Clients

Effective date: July 13, 2026

This policy describes how Creative Direct Marketing Group, Inc. ("CDMG," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information provided by our clients and prospective clients, including information submitted through the forms on this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us, request a consultation, or engage CDMG, we may collect business contact information such as your name, company, title, phone number, email address, and mailing address; information about your products, goals, and marketing programs that you share with us; and billing and engagement information needed to perform our services.

How we use it

We use client information to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our services, administer client engagements, communicate about work in progress, and share marketing insights such as the Direct Marketing Update. We do not sell client information.

Confidentiality of client business information

Information about your business, campaigns, results, and strategy that you share with CDMG in the course of an engagement is treated as confidential and used only to perform the work, except where you authorize otherwise (for example, an agreed case study or testimonial) or where disclosure is required by law.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers who support our operations (such as production, mailing, media, and technology vendors) under obligations consistent with this policy, and where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Retention and security

We retain client information for as long as needed to serve the engagement and meet legal and accounting requirements, and we use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect it.

Your choices and contact

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time, and you may contact us to review, correct, or request deletion of your information: info@cdmginc.com, 615.933.4647, or CDMG, 1313 4th Ave North, Nashville, TN 37208. For information practices relating to consumers reached by campaigns we produce, see our Privacy Policy for Consumers.
