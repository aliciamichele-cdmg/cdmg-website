Marketing for Regulation A+ offerings

Regulation A+ lets companies raise capital from the general public, which makes the marketing the offering's engine: the raise only goes as far as investor acquisition can carry it. It is also one of the most compliance-sensitive assignments in advertising, because every communication is governed by SEC rules for the offering stage it runs in. CDMG brings four decades of direct response investor marketing to exactly this problem.

How Regulation A+ marketing works

A Reg A+ campaign moves through stages, and the marketing must match each one: "test the waters" communications to gauge interest before qualification, then live-offering campaigns that drive investors to the offering circular once the SEC qualifies the offering. Required legends, disclaimers, and links to the offering materials are not optional add-ons; they are part of the creative architecture from the first draft.

What CDMG brings to a capital raise

  • Investor-audience targeting. Through InfoMat, our in-house data company established in 1978, CDMG selects and models investor-responsive audiences rather than renting generic reach.
  • Long-form persuasion for considered decisions. Investing is the ultimate considered purchase. CDMG's long-form direct response formats present the company, the story, the terms, and the risks in the way sophisticated prospects actually evaluate them.
  • Multichannel acquisition. Direct mail, digital, email, and accountable media working as one system, tracked to cost per investor and average investment, not clicks.
  • Compliance discipline. Creative built to run inside SEC communication rules, in coordination with the issuer's securities counsel at every stage.

Measured like every CDMG campaign

A capital raise has unforgiving math: cost per investor acquired against average check size. CDMG structures Reg A+ campaigns the same way it has structured direct response campaigns since 1985: test the message and the audience, measure the response, scale the winners. The result is a raise where every marketing dollar is accountable to the round.

Common questions

Can you advertise a Regulation A+ offering?

Yes. Regulation A+ permits general solicitation, including 'test the waters' communications before qualification and public marketing of the live offering after SEC qualification, provided each communication carries the required legends and links to the offering materials. Marketing must be coordinated with securities counsel.

What is 'test the waters' marketing?

Communications made before or during SEC review to gauge investor interest in a planned offering. They carry specific required disclaimers and collect indications of interest rather than investments, giving issuers a measured read on demand before the raise goes live.

What does investor acquisition cost in a Reg A+ raise?

It varies by story, terms, and audience, which is why the campaign is built around testing. The controlling metric is cost per investor against average investment size, and CDMG structures campaigns so that math is visible and improving throughout the raise.

Why use a direct response agency for a capital raise?

Because a Reg A+ raise is a direct response problem: a defined audience must be moved to a specific, measurable action under strict rules. That is the discipline CDMG has practiced for four decades in regulated categories, from financial publishers to investment products.
