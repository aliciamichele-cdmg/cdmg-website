Privacy Policy for Consumers

Effective date: July 13, 2026

This policy describes how Creative Direct Marketing Group, Inc. ("CDMG," "we," "us") handles personal information of consumers: visitors to this website and individuals who receive marketing communications produced by CDMG on behalf of its clients.

Visitors to this website

If you submit a form on cdmginc.com, we collect the information you provide (such as name, phone, and email) to respond to your inquiry. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics may automatically log technical information such as browser type, pages visited, and general usage patterns to operate and improve the site.

Consumers reached by client campaigns

CDMG creates marketing campaigns for its clients. When a campaign is mailed or delivered to you, the sender identified in that communication is responsible for the offer and, in most cases, controls the customer relationship and its data. Audience data used for targeting is obtained through lawful list and data channels, including our affiliated list brokerage InfoMat, and is used for the campaign purposes for which it was provided.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. We do not use consumer information for purposes unrelated to the marketing services we provide.

Your choices

You may opt out of communications from CDMG at any time using the contact information below. If you wish to stop receiving mail from a specific CDMG client, the fastest path is the contact or opt-out mechanism in that client's communication; you may also contact us and we will direct your request appropriately. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, which you can exercise by contacting us.

Contact

Email info@cdmginc.com, call 615.933.4647, or write to CDMG, 1313 4th Ave North, Nashville, TN 37208. For our practices regarding client business information, see the Privacy Policy for Clients.
