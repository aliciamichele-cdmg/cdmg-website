Compliant product marketing, made easy

Compliant product marketing is advertising built for regulated and compliance-sensitive categories, where the creative must produce a measurable response and stay within the rules that govern what can be claimed, how it is substantiated, and what must be disclosed. It is the discipline CDMG has practiced since 1985, and it is the promise in our name: compliant product marketing made easy.

What is compliant product marketing?

Compliant product marketing combines direct response copy and creative with the regulatory requirements of the category being advertised: FDA and FTC rules for supplements and consumer health products, SEC and FINRA considerations for financial and investment offers, and state and federal requirements for insurance and energy. The goal is a campaign that converts and clears review, because persuasive marketing that cannot ship is worth nothing.

Why regulated brands work with CDMG

  • Four decades in regulated categories. CDMG has built campaigns for consumer health, nutritional supplements, financial publishers, insurance, and energy since 1985.
  • Claims that are substantiated before they run. Copy is written with the required disclosures and review built in, not bolted on after legal sees it.
  • Accountable results. Every campaign is tracked, tested, and tied to response, so compliance never becomes an excuse for marketing that does not perform.
  • In-house targeting data. Our sister company InfoMat, established in 1978, gives CDMG audience data and list intelligence most agencies have to outsource.

How compliant campaigns still win

The most common myth in regulated marketing is that compliance kills response. Our experience across more than 40 years shows the opposite: specificity, proof, and transparency are what compliance requires, and they are also what makes direct response copy convert. Campaigns for brands including WeightWatchers, Humana, and 1-800 Contacts were built on exactly that combination of persuasion and precision.

Common questions

What categories need compliant product marketing?

Dietary supplements and nutraceuticals, consumer health products, financial products and newsletters, investment offerings, insurance, energy, and any category where the FDA, FTC, SEC, or state regulators govern advertising claims and disclosures.

Does compliance reduce marketing response?

No. The specificity and substantiation that compliance requires are the same qualities that make direct response copy convert. In CDMG's experience across four decades, compliant campaigns built on proof and transparency outperform vague, hype-driven creative.

How does CDMG keep campaigns compliant?

Claims are grounded in substantiation from the start, required disclosures are designed into the creative rather than appended to it, and campaigns go through review before launch. The result is marketing that performs and clears legal.

What is an example of a compliance-sensitive campaign CDMG runs?

Customer acquisition for a dietary supplement brand: the copy must persuade without making disease claims, carry FDA-required disclaimers, and satisfy FTC substantiation standards, while still producing a measurable, profitable response.
