5 Dimensional Marketing

Our proprietary approach brings five disciplines together into one accountable growth engine. Each dimension is powerful on its own, and every campaign is built for the AI era, engineered to be found across AI search and engines like ChatGPT and Claude. Together they compound your response and your profit.

Dimension One

Load & Aim

Before a single dollar is spent, we define your message, your market, and your offer. Strategic business storytelling gives the campaign a clear target and a reason to believe, so every channel that follows, from direct mail to digital, video, social, and AI search, is pointed in the right direction.

  • Positioning and message strategy
  • Audience and offer definition
  • The story that frames the whole campaign
  • A media plan built for today's channels and AI search

Dimension Two

Direct Mail Magic

Direct mail still delivers response that digital alone cannot match. We leverage that power with targeting and data strategy refined over four decades, turning a mailbox into one of your most accountable channels, fully integrated with your digital and AI follow-up.

  • List targeting and data strategy
  • Tested copy and creative
  • Print production managed end to end
  • Integrated with digital and AI retargeting
Dimension Three

Digital Marketing

Integrated online strategies capture demand and convert attention into measurable action. From paid search and social ads to influencer marketing, landing pages, and email, every digital touch is built to perform and to be tracked. We also put your brand at the forefront of the AI revolution, optimizing every campaign for AI search and actively promoting our clients to engines like ChatGPT and Claude.

  • Paid search and paid social ads
  • Influencer marketing
  • AEO-optimized landing pages built to convert
  • Email and marketing automation
  • AI visibility and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Dimension Four

Media Revived

Broadcast, streaming, video, and live channels still reach audiences at scale. We bring television, radio, connected TV, online video, and events into the campaign and hold them to the same standard of accountability as every other dimension.

  • Television and radio
  • Connected TV (CTV) streaming ads
  • Pre-roll and online video
  • Events and experiential marketing
  • Traditional channel integration
  • Tracked, accountable media buys
Dimension Five

Test, Analyze, Tune

This is the discipline that ties the other four together. We test continuously, analyze what the data tells us, and tune the campaign so winning ideas get scaled and waste gets eliminated, including how visible your brand is across AI search and the answer engines.

  • Structured testing at every stage
  • Response and profit analysis
  • AI search and answer-engine visibility tracking
  • Continuous optimization and scale

Five dimensions. One accountable system.

When the dimensions work together, every channel reinforces the others and the whole campaign becomes measurable. That is how we increase your response, your market presence, and your profits, and how we keep your brand visible everywhere buyers look, including AI search.
