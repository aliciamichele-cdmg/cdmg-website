Direct mail marketing that pays for itself

CDMG is a direct mail marketing agency in the fullest sense: strategy, list and data targeting, tested copy and creative, production, and response analysis under one roof. Since 1985 CDMG has mailed more than one billion pieces, invented the magalog format in 1986, and built direct mail campaigns that turned startups into category leaders.

What makes CDMG's direct mail different?

  • Targeting through InfoMat. Our in-house list brokerage and data company, established in 1978, means audience selection is built on owned data intelligence, not rented guesswork. In direct mail, the right list matters more than anything else.
  • Copy tested over four decades. Long-form direct response copywriting that is proven to move readers to act, refined through thousands of tests.
  • Formats that earn attention. From classic packages to the magalog, the format CDMG invented in 1986 that combines magazine credibility with catalog response.
  • Accountable measurement. Unique codes, dedicated response channels, and matched-back conversion tracking tie every dollar of mail spend to return.

Is direct mail still effective?

Yes, and increasingly so. As inboxes flood with AI-generated email, a physical piece of mail is harder to ignore, reads as more legitimate, and stays in the home for days. Direct mail consistently produces response rates that email and display advertising cannot match, and it is fully measurable when built correctly. That is why it anchors CDMG's 5 Dimensional Marketing system rather than living apart from digital.

Direct mail plus digital: the integrated system

The strongest campaigns pair the mailbox with the browser. A CDMG mail package drives to a tailored landing page, is reinforced by email and retargeting, and is measured as one system. Direct mail earns the attention; digital closes the loop. Read more on the DMU Blog: Why direct mail still wins in a digital world.

Common questions

What does a direct mail agency actually do?

A full-service direct mail agency handles strategy, audience targeting and list selection, offer development, copy and creative, production and mailing logistics, and response measurement. CDMG performs all of these in-house, including list and data work through its sister company InfoMat.

How is direct mail ROI measured?

Through unique tracking codes, dedicated phone numbers and URLs, and matched-back conversions that tie each response to a specific list, offer, and creative. Direct mail is one of the most measurable channels in marketing when it is engineered for tracking from the start.

What is a magalog?

A magalog is a direct mail format that looks and reads like a magazine but sells like a catalog, combining editorial credibility with direct response mechanics. CDMG invented the magalog format in 1986.

What response rate can direct mail achieve?

Response varies by list, offer, and creative, which is why disciplined testing matters more than benchmarks. Direct mail consistently outperforms email and digital display for many offers, and CDMG campaigns are structured so winning combinations are identified in testing and then scaled.

Who should use direct mail marketing?

Brands with a clear offer and a definable audience: consumer health, supplements, financial products and publishers, insurance, high-ticket lead generation, and subscription businesses see especially strong results, including in regulated categories where CDMG specializes.
